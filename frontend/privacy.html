Privacy Policy

Your privacy is paramount. Civic Waste Reporter is built with privacy-first architecture, ensuring that citizen reports remain safe, local, and transparent.

1. Information We Collect

Civic Waste Reporter collects only the minimal information strictly required to identify civic waste and prepare an official grievance dispatch for municipal authorities:

  • Incident Photographs: Photos of uncollected garbage or illegal dumping submitted directly by the user.
  • Geolocation Coordinates: Latitude and longitude coordinates chosen by the user on the map or extracted from the photo's EXIF metadata to determine the responsible municipal ward or corporation.
  • Report Timestamps: Date and time when the waste incident was documented.

2. Automatic Privacy Protection & PII Redaction

Before any photograph is analyzed or attached, our server automatically runs local computer vision algorithms (Haar Cascades) to detect and redact sensitive Personally Identifiable Information (PII):

  • Human faces are blurred with a heavy 51×51 Gaussian filter.
  • Vehicle license plates are blurred to protect bystanders and vehicle owners.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy & Gmail Scope

Our application integrates with the Google Gmail API to provide a one-click draft creation workflow. We request only the following scope:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.compose

How this scope is used:

  • This permission is used solely to compose an email draft in the user's Gmail account addressed to the appropriate municipal authority, pre-filling the complaint letter and attaching the sanitized incident photograph.
  • We NEVER read your emails, access your inbox, modify existing emails, or delete messages.
  • We NEVER send emails automatically. The user maintains 100% control to review the draft inside Gmail and manually press Send.
Google Limited Use Compliance: Civic Waste Reporter's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Retention & Third-Party Sharing

  • No Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data, email addresses, or images to any third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.
  • Temporary Retention: Uploaded evidence photos and draft sessions are held in process-local memory temporarily (maximum 24 hours) solely to allow the Gmail draft creation flow, after which they are automatically purged.
  • No Persistent User Accounts: The service does not require password registration or retain tracking profiles.

5. Managing Your Google Permissions

You can review or revoke Civic Waste Reporter's permission to create Gmail drafts at any time by visiting your Google Account Security Permissions.
